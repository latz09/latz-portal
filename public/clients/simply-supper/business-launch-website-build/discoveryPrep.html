Discovery Questions

🎯
Notes to self — not a script Annie already told us the business model, the values, and the feature wishlist unprompted — don't re-ask any of that. Focus on the integration question first; it's the one thing that could swing this from a standard subscription site to something a lot more involved.
Integration & Third-Party System — Start Here
Who ends up owning customer accounts and payment — the website, or the software the other developer is building?
Already asked in the first email, but worth revisiting live. This decides whether the site is a full subscription/e-commerce platform (accounts, billing, order history) or a marketing front end that hands off to someone else's system.
What is that developer building this in, and will it expose an API or webhook the website can talk to?
If it's closed with no way in, real integration (auto-syncing orders, pan status, inventory) isn't realistic — it'd mean manual exports or a much looser relationship between the two systems. This is the detail that changes the level of effort the most.
Did she decide who she's going with, and how did that meeting go?
That meeting was scheduled for the day after this chat — by the time discovery happens, this may already be settled.
Does she want a second opinion on that system's fit with the website, or is that track already locked in on her end?
Since it directly shapes scope and price, worth knowing whether Jordan has any input there or if it's simply a constraint to design around.
Business & Goals
What does success look like in year one — subscriber count, delivery radius?
Helps size the site. A hyper-local pilot launch needs less than a "we're taking sign-ups region-wide" launch.
Is delivery starting Stevens Point/central WI only, or wider from day one?
Determines whether a delivery-zone or zip-code checker needs to exist at launch.
Content & Structure
What pages does she picture — menu previews, sign-up flow, gifting flow, an about/story page, a future cooking-classes page?
Builds the sitemap. Gifting and college-membership flows are distinct enough from a normal sign-up that they probably need their own dedicated paths.
Is the messaging in her email meant as final copy direction, or just tone guidance?
She gave strong, specific language (empowered/hopeful/healthy, caring for family, reducing environmental impact, local community) — confirm whether that's meant to show up close to verbatim or just inform the voice.
Does she have professional photos of the meals and packaging, or does that need to be arranged?
Food photography carries a lot of the weight on a meal-delivery site. If none exist, that's a pre-launch dependency to flag early.
Design & Brand
Is the current logo final, or is she open to revisiting it?
The logo reads friendly and homey; her messaging is more mission-driven around sustainability and community. Worth gently asking whether the visual identity should evolve to carry that weight, or whether she's happy keeping it as-is.
Any color or material preferences beyond the logo — for the totes, packaging, or delivery materials?
Ties the physical reusable-container experience to the digital brand.
Decisions, Updates & Practical
How comfortable is she updating the site herself — weekly menus will presumably change often?
Determines whether she needs a CMS she can run herself, or wants Jordan handling ongoing content updates.
Is it just her deciding, or is anyone else involved in decisions?
Standard, but worth confirming — no mention of a co-owner or partner so far.
Any timeline pressure — a launch season or target date?
CSA-style businesses often launch around a season. Worth knowing if there's a date driving urgency.
Budget range in mind?
Frame as scoping help, not a gotcha — especially important here since the integration question could swing the estimate meaningfully.
