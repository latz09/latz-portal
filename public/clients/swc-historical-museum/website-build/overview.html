Their eight pain points collapse into five jobs. Every scope decision should trace to one of these. Anything that does not is optional.

  • 2️⃣
    Let Alison post an event in minutes, so she stops defaulting to Facebook.
  • 3️⃣
    Take a card, online and at the front desk, in one system.
  • 4️⃣

  • 5️⃣

    Not always top of Google when someone searches for them by name. Alison cannot see website visitor numbers, only a monthly Google report on searches, and said she was not sure how to find out.

Notes & Observations

The board needs convincing, not just a quote

Some board members are elderly and a few are not online at all. The three presenters asked Jordan to help sell why branding and a modern site matter. He offered the 80-year-old fishing club story as an example, plus live links to the Hewitt site and CWED. Write that section in plain language for people who do not use the web.

"Archives can wait" does not mean the Artifacts PDFs can

They put the archive and digital collections on the back burner, but Alison also said most of their search hits come when Google picks up a keyword inside the Artifacts issues on the site. Phase 1 must keep those PDFs reachable at their current addresses or redirect them. Preserving them is included in the base price. Ask for the Search Console data once access exists.

Alison is the daily user and has little time

No web background, inherited the site, and spends her effort on Facebook. The CMS has to be dead simple: add an event, feature it, publish a newsletter post. If the site goes stale again, the project failed even if the launch looks great.

Mary brings real marketing and design experience

She spent a career as a marketing and communications director and has worked with designers for decades. Expect specific feedback on brand and design. She and Meg were the ones who first offered to help Alison with the website, and this idea grew out of that a few weeks ago.

Meg missed the first 20 minutes

She came in at about the 20 minute mark, after roles, board process, site ownership and the pain points. She did hear the payments, newsletter and brand kit discussion. Send her a short recap with the proposal so nothing surprises her in front of the board.

Outside designer question is live

It was raised late in the call, and one person said they are talking to different people. Jordan was open to their person doing the logo and brand, with Alyssa on the website. Hold the brand quote as its own line so either path works, and revisit after Thanksgiving.

Do not read the 990 as cash

Prep doc: FY2025 revenue looks large, but most of the net assets are the building, collection and investments, and earlier years ran deficits. The jump may be a one-time gift. This is a volunteer nonprofit with a brand-new budget request, so price honestly and let the options do the work.

Tone

Friendly, gracious and a little self-deprecating about the site ("you're not in 1998"). They like history and love showing off the building. Keep proposals warm and plain, direct about cost, and do not pitch past what they raised.
