Watch-Outs for This Call

⚠ Don't Quote Live
Ask for their range first. If pressed, give a range with the assumptions stated and say the firm quote follows by Wednesday. The ladder numbers are estimates, not your rates; adjust before the call.
⚠ Don't Read the 990 as Cash
FY2025 shows $506,782 revenue vs $176,084 expenses and $2.12M net assets, but most of that is building, collection, and investments. Earlier years ran deficits. The jump may be a one-time gift. Ask, don't assume.
⚠ Two Domains, One Email Risk
Site is swch-museum.com, staff email is on southwoodcountyhistory.org. Don't promise any domain or DNS move until you know who owns each and where email is hosted.
⚠ Brand + SEO Scope
Don't commit to a full brand system price before Alyssa confirms what's in the kit. Don't promise rankings; SEO is the built-in foundation.
⚠ Don't Pitch
They came to you through Pookie. Let them tell the story. Don't raise Letters From Wildwood unless Meg does.
⚠ Zoom Logistics
If Meg never confirmed Zoom vs Meet, use the Zoom link you already sent. If you're on Basic, say up front it cuts at 40 minutes and everyone rejoins with the same link.
