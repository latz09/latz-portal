A Note From Jordan

Stevens Point, WI

Jeff,

Phase 1's homepage does a lot in a small space — that "you talk to me directly" line is one of the more effective pieces on the whole site. It's just doing too much with too little room. Your story, why you run things this way, the partnerships behind every build — none of that fits in a homepage sidebar.

The wireframe I sent shows what a full page could look like — six sections, each one a placeholder for now. Things like your story and the 22 years that got you here, why you've kept this small and hands-on instead of building out a bigger team, the mill and craftsman network behind every home, and an invite to come see the Pittsville showroom in person. Those are just examples of what could go where — the actual headings and order will follow whatever you send.

This is exactly the kind of page that sets you apart from builders hiding behind a sales team. It just needs somewhere real to live.

Once you're ready, all I need is rough content for each section — bullet points, half-sentences, whatever comes to mind — and I'll shape it into the actual copy and headings. Here's what it takes to get this built.

— Jordan
