01

A Note From Jordan

Stevens Point, WI

Jeff,

The floor plan site's been live for a while now and doing its job — no complaints there. But the price calculator's been down since Elementor Pro's license lapsed, which is a strange thing to lose a working tool over: the calculator itself was never the licensed part, it just happened to render through a widget that needed one to function at all.

I pulled the old plugin apart before writing a word of this — the code, the database, the actual pricing data, not a guess based on what it looks like from the outside. It's not a simple form. It's a real bill-of-materials estimator: log type and size, wall dimensions, corners, gable logs, floor beams, joists, rafters, purlins, ridge beams, collar ties, posts, caulk, gasket, screws, stairs, porch, loft floor, ceiling — about 15 pricing categories, each priced and summed into one real number. You confirmed all of it's actually in use, by you and by your customers, so none of it gets trimmed in the rebuild.

While I was in there I found two things worth fixing along the way. Corner pricing ran a formula through PHP's eval() — a real security anti-pattern, and unnecessary besides, since only two corner types exist. That gets replaced with real logic. And the porch price field looked editable in the old admin but was silently overridden by a hardcoded value in the code — it never actually worked. Both get fixed as part of this, no extra charge.

What matters most going forward: you want to add, edit, and delete pricing items yourself, not just tweak numbers on the ones that already exist. That's the whole point of the rebuild — a calculator that's actually yours, with nothing licensed anywhere in it to expire on you again.

— Jordan
