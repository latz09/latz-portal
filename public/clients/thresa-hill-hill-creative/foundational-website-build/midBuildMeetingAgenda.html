03 — Design Direction

Let's nail down the "breaking up the scroll" feel.

Back when you first saw the preview, you mentioned the page felt a little blocky — sections stacked back to back with nothing breaking things up. Good instinct, and an easy fix. Here's where that idea stands.

What's been floated so far — nothing built yet

Opening up the three-service section on the homepage by softening the solid color blocks behind it, and adding breathing room between sections with things like short testimonial quotes, a pull-quote banner, or a simple stat callout (homes photographed, clients served, that kind of thing). Your own mockup already has a nice example of this — the quote banner and testimonial layout — worth a quick look together today as a reference point.

What we need from you: a thumbs up on this direction (or your own spin on it), so we can build it and push it straight to the live preview instead of guessing.
