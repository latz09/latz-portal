Not Sure Where Your Domain Lives? Here's How to Find Out.

  1. Search your email for a receipt or renewal notice mentioning hillcreativellc.com. Likely senders: GoDaddy, Namecheap, or Squarespace.
  2. Check a card statement for a small recurring charge, usually $10 to $20 a year, from one of those same companies.
  3. Go to who.is and type hillcreativellc.com into the search bar. That will show you who the domain is registered with.
  4. Last resort: reply to the email this came with, and Jordan will track it down for you.
