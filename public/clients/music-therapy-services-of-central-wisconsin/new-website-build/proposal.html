A Note From Jordan

Stevens Point, WI

Elizabeth,

Twenty-five years ago, Music Therapy Services of Central Wisconsin opened its doors, and it's been earning trust with families ever since — trust the website flat-out doesn't reflect. It's still running on the same Turbify build from back then: locked down so tight visitors can't even pinch-zoom, tabs that put "Get Started" right next to "Contact" with no clear reason to pick one, and nothing you can touch yourselves when something needs to change.

That's a real cost for a practice like yours, because this site has two jobs, not one. A parent looking into care for a kid with developmental or neurological needs is sizing you up fast, and needs to trust what they're seeing. And with a waitlist as long as yours, you're also trying to recruit therapists faster than you can hire them — right now, that ask is buried in a tab instead of leading the page. A site stuck in 2006 can't do either job well, let alone both.

I put both of those front and center in the wireframe I sent alongside this — the homepage forks right at the hero, one path for families, one for the therapists you're trying to bring on. Nothing generic here; it's built around the actual problem you're solving.

We're both Stevens Point, which makes this easy — I'm not a call center three states away, and neither are you. Here's what it takes to get mtscw.com off Turbify and onto something you own outright.

— Jordan
