What is Music Therapy? CMS editable
current copy · tightened

Music is processed all across the brain — not in one single spot — which makes it a powerful way to work around damaged areas and build new connections. In a session, kids and adults might play instruments, sing, write songs, or move to music. No musical experience or talent is needed to benefit.

Our Story CMS editable
current copy · tightened

Music Therapy Services of Central Wisconsin began in 1999 and has helped thousands of individuals and groups across Central Wisconsin. We work to improve quality of life through music — especially for children and adults with developmental or neurological needs, illnesses, or life-changing events that affect their independence, connection, or comfort.

Your story — 25+ years serving central Wisconsin, women-owned, and the mission behind the work. We’ll help you expand it a little from the current version.
Our Approach CMS editable
current copy · tightened

Music therapy is an established, credentialed health profession: music used within a therapeutic relationship to reach functional, non-musical goals. Every technique is chosen from clinical and scientific evidence, targeting rehabilitative, adaptive, and developmental goals.

How music therapy works and why it’s effective — the evidence-based side that builds confidence for families and referring providers alike.
