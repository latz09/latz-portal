02

Photo Guidance

🛡️
Any photo with a child needs a signed release first Before any session photo, team photo, or homepage image featuring a child can go live on the site, we'll need a signed photo release from that child's parent or guardian on file. If you already collect these, great — just make sure the ones covering any photos you send us are in hand. If you're not sure what you have, we're happy to help you put a simple release together.

👍 Great Shots

  • Original files, not screenshots
  • Horizontal orientation for anything used as a hero
  • Natural light — near a window works great
  • Candid moments over posed, stiff shots
  • Wide enough that we can crop if needed

👎 Try to Avoid

  • Facebook or Instagram screenshots
  • Anything blurry or heavily filtered
  • Very dark or backlit shots
  • Vertical-only photos for hero spots
  • Stock photos — real photos build more trust
