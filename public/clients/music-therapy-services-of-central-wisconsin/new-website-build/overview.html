A few small factual claims need fixing before anything goes public: the site draft says "over 30 years" but the practice was founded in 1999 (~27 years), and the accessibility statement currently reads like it's claiming "the highest standard" when AA — not AAA — is what's actually being followed. Both are easy fixes, but worth catching before launch rather than after, given this client's field.

Neurologic Music Therapy® and NMT® are both now registered marks — acknowledged to Elizabeth by email, but not yet confirmed as updated in the internal "NMT Rules" content reference. Also still need to check whether NMT-F™ is a separate registered mark from NMT® before finalizing any copy that uses it.

Jul 28, 2026 — Post-Discovery (carried forward)

Elizabeth is friendly, easy, and a genuine champion — but defers to Richelle on substance. Keep deliverables simple enough that she can present them cleanly on her own.

Local Angle

She specifically wanted a LOCAL designer. Jordan being in Stevens Point landed well — lean into that in design-phase conversations too.

Design Challenge — Resolved, Now Being Refined

The dual audience was the whole project: "fun, colorful, kid-friendly" coexisting with "professional, clinical, evidence-based." Alyssa's design struck that balance and Elizabeth approved the homepage on 10/5 — her 11/2 feedback is fine-tuning within that same approved direction (fewer colors, less motion), not reopening it.
