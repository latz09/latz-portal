Notes & Observations

Aug 25, 2026 — Post-Deposit

The approval chain here runs through two people with very different response speeds. Elizabeth is fast and easy — often same-day. Richelle, as the actual budget holder, moves slower by nature and this round was further slowed by a medical leave. Anything needing Richelle's direct action (payment, formal sign-off) should get a longer runway than Elizabeth's responsiveness would suggest. Build that into expectations for the balance payment at launch.

Jul 28, 2026 — Post-Discovery (carried forward)

Elizabeth is friendly, easy, and a genuine champion — but defers to Richelle on substance. Keep deliverables simple enough that she can present them cleanly on her own.

Local Angle

She specifically wanted a LOCAL designer. Jordan being in Stevens Point landed well — lean into that in design-phase conversations too.

Design Challenge

The dual audience is the whole project. "Fun, colorful, kid-friendly" has to coexist with "professional, clinical, evidence-based." Still need Elizabeth's inspiration examples to anchor the balance before design directions are built.
