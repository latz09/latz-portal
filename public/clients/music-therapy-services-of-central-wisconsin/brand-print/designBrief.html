Project Snapshot

The job

This grew directly out of the website rebuild — Elizabeth wants every client-facing piece to match, not just the site. Right now nothing is urgently broken about MTSCW's print materials, but nothing matches either: a brochure, business cards, and a social presence that were never built as one system. This package fixes that, right as the new site goes live. Winning looks like a brochure Elizabeth can hand a referring professional without a second thought, business cards covering a growing team off one swappable design, and a social presence — banners and icons — that reads as the same brand wherever someone finds it.

Brand & Visual Direction

🎨
Same starting point as the site — tied directly to it The plan is to keep the client's existing logo and brand blue, brightened, carried over from the website decisions. The logo files are already in hand — in the shared Google Drive asset folder for this project, same place as the rest of the client assets. One more thing worth knowing before anything goes to print: Neurologic Music Therapy® is a registered mark, and the acronym NMT® is now registered too — the client's own brand reference doc is out of date on that. Whether the Fellow-level mark (NMT-F™) is affected hasn't been fully confirmed either, so it's worth a quick check before any credential text gets locked into something as hard to revise as a print run.

Open Design Questions
How many brochures does Elizabeth actually want, and does she want a digital PDF too?
Still open from the original quote conversation — worth nailing down before a layout gets locked, since it changes how much content the piece needs to carry.
Which years-of-experience figure is correct for Richelle — 25+ or 30+?
Both numbers show up across different client materials, and the practice itself was founded in 1999 — worth settling on one before it's printed somewhere as permanent as a brochure.
Does the musical-note motif carry onto print, or does print stay its own quieter expression of the same palette?
The site leans into that motif for Direction 2 — curious whether you want it echoed here or kept web-only.
How should MT-BC / NMT® / NMT-F™ actually be formatted and spaced on something as small as a business card?
NMT® just became a registered mark on top of Neurologic Music Therapy®, and the Fellow-level mark hasn't been fully confirmed — worth settling the exact string before it's locked into something expensive to redo.
Which platforms actually get a banner?
Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn have all come up in conversation, but nothing's confirmed — matters for sizing before you start.
