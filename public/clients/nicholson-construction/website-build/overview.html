Positioning & The Blocker

🧱
THE ONE BLOCKER: how do we present work he didn't solely build? The entire existing photo library (10 Drive folders) is Sand Valley builds Billy contributed to alongside other contractors — often as a sub. He's uncomfortable implying "we solely built these," but still wants to show off work that "trumps most around." Everything else is downstream of solving this.
Recommended solution: flip the constraint into the flex.

Position Nicholson as "the crew the best builders bring in when the work has to be right." Add a small, honest "Our role" line to every project — e.g. "Full custom build," "Decorative concrete + wood exteriors," "Framing & exterior in partnership with [builder], Sand Valley." Specifics read as MORE credible than vague claims, legitimize using the Sand Valley photos, keep the ego small, and quietly signal to contractor partners that he's partner-caliber.
