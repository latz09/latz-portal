Move hosting into your name

Before your site can go live, hosting needs to run under your own account and your own card — not ours. Three quick steps, about 5 minutes total.

Time needed ~5 minutes

Why this matters

You own this website, so you should own the hosting it runs on too. Once billing is set up in your name, there's no monthly fee to Latz Web Design — hosting bills directly to you, and the account is fully yours.

This step doesn't touch the build or the timeline. It's a billing handoff. Once it's confirmed on your end, we'll connect palsparkwi.com and take the site live.

1

Accept your invite

Check your email for an invite to a Vercel team called Pal's Park. Click accept, then create an account or log in if you already have one.

2

Add your card

Inside the Pal's Park team, go to Settings → Billing → Add Card and enter your payment information.

3

Set it as default

Mark the new card as your default payment method so it's the one billed going forward.

You're done

That's your part. Hosting now bills to your own Vercel account, under your own control — you decide what happens with it going forward.

Next, we'll connect palsparkwi.com and take Pal's Park live. We'll be in touch once that's ready.
