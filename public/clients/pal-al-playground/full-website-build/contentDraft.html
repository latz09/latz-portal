Founders' Story — "We're the Hewetts"Final copy
👨‍👩‍👧Photo of Alex & Allison

Pal's Park was born from a simple wish as parents of our four-year-old: a place to play, jump, and be a kid all year round, no matter the weather.

Deep Roots

Allison's great-great-grandfather came to Wisconsin Rapids in the 1850s and established himself as a barber, one of the earliest trades that knit a community together. The generations since have become attorneys, cranberry growers, community leaders, and philanthropists who shaped, and continue to shape, the civic and agricultural life of the Wisconsin Rapids area. Allison grew up understanding that showing up, being involved, and meeting the needs of a changing community make the world a better place.

The advice from a grandfather she never met was to "do anything you want, as long as you can support yourself and live with no regrets." She did just that: left for college in Montana and worked seasonally for about 8 years as a ski instructor and wilderness trip leader. She's been back in the area full time since 2017, thanks to the gentle tug of family business and meeting Alex two years prior.

Alex's family arrived at the turn of the twentieth century when his great-grandfather, an immigrant from Denmark, opened a bakery and began building something of his own. The generations that followed became nurses, teachers, millwrights, and artists. College and law school took him out to Washington, DC, but his home state called him back. Since moving back home to Wisconsin Rapids in 2015, Alex has advised hundreds of clients and businesses on many different legal topics and issues.

Why An Indoor Playground?

We believe Wisconsin Rapids is a great place to raise a family and enjoy the outdoors. We also know winters can be long, and the weather (any time of year) is unpredictable. We also believe kids, and parents, need a real break from screens, a place to move, play, and connect face to face instead. We hope this playground fills a void in the community, and this can feel like your third option other than home or school. We plan to be open every day of the year so we can shake our sillies out, no matter the weather, and even on a holiday.

Together, we are part of this community for the long haul. Pal's Park is the next chapter of two families that have been building things here for more than a century.

FAQFinal copy
🕐
What are your hours?
Sunday through Thursday, 8am to 8pm. Friday and Saturday, 8am to 9pm. Open every day of the year, including holidays.
🧩
What is a self-serve playground?
Pal's Park operates on a self-serve model, designed to give families flexibility while keeping safety a top priority: purchase a Day Pass or Membership online and get your own entrance code; a parent or guardian (18+) stays with their child the entire visit — kids can't be dropped off; our team is on-site for cleaning and maintenance, though supervision stays with the accompanying adult; adults may not enter without a child; and common areas are monitored by security cameras.
🔒
How is Pal's Park kept secure?
An electronic liability waiver at checkout, a unique entrance code per purchase, security cameras at entry points and throughout the park, and owners/attendants on-site for cleaning and maintenance.
🧦
Do I need socks or shoes?
Clean athletic shoes or socks are required throughout the park, with socks only permitted on the jumping pillow. Bare feet aren't allowed, and shoes are highly recommended. Socks are available in our vending machine if you forget yours.
🧼
How is the park cleaned?
Pal's Park follows Wisconsin Department of Health guidelines for indoor play area sanitation, with a daily, weekly, and monthly cleaning schedule covering everything from high-touch surfaces to full play equipment sanitizing.
🤒
Can sick children come to play?
To help keep Pal's Park healthy for everyone, we ask that children stay home for at least 24 hours after a fever, vomiting, or other contagious symptoms have resolved.
