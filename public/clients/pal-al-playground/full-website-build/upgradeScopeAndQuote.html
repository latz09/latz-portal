On-Brand Auto-Reply EmailsOptional · $500

Everyone who fills out a form gets an automatic, on-brand email right away: a Pal's Park styled thank-you that confirms their message or application came through. It covers multiple forms (Contact, Sponsor, and Equity Access), each with its own tailored message, so the price is one flat $500 and not per form.

Why it's worth it
  • Peace of mind. People know it went through, so you get fewer "did you get my form?" follow-ups.
  • Especially for Equity Access. A family applying for help should hear back immediately that their application was received.
  • Sets expectations. Each email can say when to expect a reply, so you're not chasing a timeline you never stated.
  • First impression. A branded email from day one makes Pal's Park feel established before the doors even open.
