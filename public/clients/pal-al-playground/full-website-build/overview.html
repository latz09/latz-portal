Notes & Observations

Business Rename — Update Everywhere

Now officially Pal's Park LLC, formerly "Hewett Indoor Playground" and informally "the playground." Make sure this is reflected consistently across contracts, collateral, and any future correspondence.

No Firm Launch Date Was Ever Committed — Watch the November Framing

Before this week, no full-site launch date had actually been committed to the client. "February" was an internal planning assumption tied to the venue's own Feb 2027 opening — never promised to Allison or Pookie as the website's go-live date. The first-week-of-November ask came from Pookie on Oct 5, not from Jordan; the real answer is still pending wireframe approval. Be careful not to let Pookie's proposed date harden into a commitment before the review is actually back.

Pookie's Role Has Expanded

Originally a structural advisor and launch-marketing stakeholder. Now confirmed as the gatekeeper for all written copy on the site — delivered the finalized, SEO-optimized "Website Copy, Final" document in September. She's also running domain registration and the Founders Club pre-sale program, which makes her the practical bottleneck on several fronts at once.

Founders Club Conflict — Resolved

Pookie had given two conflicting answers on how Founders Club money gets collected. It was flagged without assigning blame, a decision was requested with a clear deadline, and Pookie landed on a direction in early August — the Coming Soon page shipped on time with the resolved mechanism.

Content Reconciliation Caught Real Discrepancies

Building the Home + Offerings wireframe against the final copy, Jul 10 content brief, and business plan doc surfaced a few mismatches that would otherwise have shipped wrong: party room rate ($200, not $250 as floated earlier), the 10-adult party room cap, and the 2-month threshold for Founders Wall recognition. All resolved in favor of the final copy document as the authoritative source — worth remembering that these documents don't always agree with each other.

Free Scope Additions — Track Capacity

The Coming Soon page and FAQ came back into scope at no charge after being cut during negotiation. Reasonable given the relationship, and it's now shipped — but don't let more "small additions" land on top of the main site build without a conversation about scope.

Logo Process Pattern — Useful for Future Rounds

The logo took extra rounds beyond the original polish-only scope — a full font/lockup exploration detour — before settling back on the originally-recommended direction. This client explores alternatives before trusting the first good recommendation. Worth factoring into how the wireframe review and any revision rounds are framed too.

Communication Cadence — Steady

Historically 6–12 day response gaps. Since closing, the pace has held up well — content brief, photo shoot, final copy, and the Oct 2/Oct 5 status exchanges all landed without much chasing. Keep pushing for a firm answer on the punch list rather than letting "next month" linger.

Alex — Still Largely Silent

Co-owner, attorney, husband. Has not driven any decision to date, though described as "slightly more present" in recent threads. Plan communication around Allison and Pookie; don't expect Alex to weigh in unprompted, even now that the wireframe is in his inbox too.
