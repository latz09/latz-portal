Partnering Together Portage County

A community resource hub connecting Portage County families to local housing and support services.

This wireframe covers the four core pages of your new site: a homepage that welcomes both families and donors, a single Partner Organization Directory for your six founding partners, a new Community Resources page for outside organizations, and a simple way for people to reach you. Donations route out to each partner's own page — nothing to build or maintain on your end.
