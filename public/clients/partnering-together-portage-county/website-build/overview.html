Project Summary

Pain Points / Why They're Building This

  • 🔴 No central place for residents to find partner agencies, resources, and support. People currently bounce between orgs piecing it together themselves.
  • 🔴 No website exists at all today, from-scratch build.
  • 🔴 No paid staff. Whatever gets built has to be manageable by a volunteer or staff member after Jordan hands it off.

What Success Looks Like

  • ✅ A welcoming, mobile-friendly hub listing all participating partner agencies with descriptions and contact info.
  • ✅ Visitors can donate directly to any partner org's existing donation page in one click, no extra navigation friction.
  • ✅ A separate path to "learn more" about each partner org on their own site.
  • ✅ Directory structured so more partner agencies can be added later without a rebuild.
  • ✅ Management transitions to a Partnering Together staff member or volunteer within 6 months of launch.
🚫
Explicitly not in scope A unified, multi-org donation platform that splits and receipts gifts across separate 501(c)(3)s. Tabby's 11/10 email closed this out in favor of simple outbound links, she wants direct access to each org's own donation page, not a payments integration Partnering Together would have to stand up and maintain.
❓
Open questions, see "Needed from Client" Legal name to use on the proposal, Tabby's approval authority, whether other vendors are quoting this, the actual donate/learn-more URLs for each partner org, and the full list for the new Community Resources page are all still unanswered.
