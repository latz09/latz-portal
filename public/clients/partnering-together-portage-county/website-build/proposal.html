A Note From Jordan

Stevens Point, WI

Tabby,

Partnering Together Portage County doesn't have a website today, which means anyone looking for help right now has to track down six different organizations on their own, one at a time, with no single place that says "start here." For a group built specifically to bring those six organizations together, that's the one piece still missing.

This site has to do two jobs. A parent or individual looking for housing or poverty-related support needs to land on it and immediately see where to go. A donor deciding whether to give needs to trust what they're looking at before they click through to a partner's page. And since you're presenting this to your board Friday, it also needs to look like something a 501(c)(3) can stand behind, not a hobby project.

The wireframe I'm sending alongside this lays out all four pages: a homepage that welcomes both audiences, one directory for your six founding partners, a new page for outside community resources, and a simple contact page. Everything's built so you can add a partner or a resource yourself later without calling me back for a rebuild.

We're both Stevens Point, so this is an easy one to keep moving quickly, which matters with your board meeting Friday. Here's what it takes to get Partnering Together Portage County online.

— Jordan
