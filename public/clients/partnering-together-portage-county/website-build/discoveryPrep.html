Discovery Questions

🎯
Notes to self, not a script The submission already did most of the normal discovery work, scope, deliverables, even the support model, are all spelled out. Don't re-ask any of that. Focus on the things that actually change the estimate: partner agency count, donation platform, who the decision-maker is, and whether this is a competitive bid.
Business & Goals
Who is Tabby, and is she the one who can sign off on scope and budget, or does this need to go back to the board?
Role wasn't stated anywhere in the submission. The organization is run by a volunteer board, so it matters whether Tabby can move on her own or whether a proposal needs board approval before it's real.
Is there something driving the timing on this right now?
No timeline mentioned. Worth knowing if there's a grant deadline, a board meeting, or a program launch pushing it.
Are you getting quotes from other web designers for this?
The submission reads like a formal scope document, structured headers, consistent phrasing throughout. Worth knowing up front whether this is a side-by-side comparison so the proposal can be framed accordingly.
Content & Structure
Do you already have a list of partner agencies with their descriptions, contact info, and logos ready to go?
This is probably the single biggest scope variable. Determines whether this is a straightforward content upload or whether Jordan ends up chasing down info from each agency individually.
Roughly how many partner agencies are we talking about at launch, and is that expected to grow over time?
Shapes whether the directory needs to be something Tabby or staff can add to themselves after launch, or a smaller fixed set Jordan builds once.
Do you have photos and written content ready for the rest of the site, home page, about, programs, or is that still being pulled together?
Affects both timeline and whether copywriting needs to be part of the estimate.
Design & Brand
Does Partnering Together have an existing logo or any visual identity, or is this a blank slate?
Nothing in the submission mentions a logo, colors, or brand guidelines. Need to know if there's something to design around, or if that's a separate conversation, possibly one to loop Alyssa into.
Any other nonprofit sites, or any sites at all, that you like the feel of?
No visual references at all in this inquiry, unusual compared to most leads. Need something to anchor design direction before Alyssa starts anything.
Technical & Practical
Do you already have a donation platform picked out, like Stripe, Givebutter, or Network for Good, or do you want a recommendation?
Changes integration effort and whether there are transaction fees the organization should be planning around.
Is a domain already registered, and who manages email, is the Gmail address the long-term setup or just how this inquiry was sent?
partneringtogetherpc@gmail.com suggests there's no custom domain or hosted email yet. Good to confirm before scoping, since that may need to be set up as part of this project.
Decision-Making, Timeline & Budget
Who specifically is being set up to take over the site after the 6-month support window?
The submission calls this out directly as a goal. Good to know whether that's Tabby, a future hire, or a volunteer, so the training can be built around that person's actual comfort level with tech.
Is there a target launch date?
Not mentioned anywhere in the submission.
What kind of budget range are you working with?
Nothing stated. Nonprofit registry data puts total assets and income in the $100K-$500K range, mostly from one large founding contribution, so there's real capacity. But with a 100% volunteer board and no dedicated marketing budget history, they'll likely still be thinking in nonprofit rates. Frame it as helping scope the right level of work, not a gotcha.
