02

Photo Guidance

👍 Great Shots

  • Original files, not screenshots
  • Horizontal orientation for anything used as a hero
  • Natural daylight — shop door open or outdoors works great
  • Candid, in-action shots over posed lineup photos
  • Wide enough that we can crop if needed

👎 Try to Avoid

  • Facebook or Instagram screenshots
  • Anything blurry, heavily filtered, or very dark
  • Vertical-only photos for hero spots
  • Cluttered backgrounds that hide the work itself
  • Stock photos — real shots of your shop build more trust
