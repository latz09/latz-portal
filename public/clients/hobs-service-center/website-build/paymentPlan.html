Why The Total Moved

$3,500 → $3,850.

My standard terms are 50% down, balance at launch. Stretching that over 12 months instead is really a financing arrangement — I'm floating the cost of the build over a year instead of getting paid up front.

The $350 difference (10% of the base price) covers that: the cost of extending payment this way, plus card/transaction processing on thirteen separate payments instead of two. It's built into the monthly number — nothing added on top of it later.

Terms

What governs the plan.

  • 01

    Payment due date

    Monthly payment is due on the same date each month, starting one month after the down payment.

  • 02

    Grace period & late fee

    A 5-day grace period applies with no penalty. After that, a flat $25 late fee is added to the payment.

  • 03

    Missed payments

    Two missed or late payments, or 30 days past due, triggers written notice. If the balance isn't resolved by 45–60 days past due, the site is suspended (a simple "under maintenance" page, not taken offline or broken).

  • 04

    Ownership on completion

    Domain/hosting transfer, full CMS admin access, and final source files are handed over once the balance is paid in full — not before.

Everything else stays standard. Custom build, no templates, mobile-first, built to convert — same as every Latz Web Design project. The payment plan changes how it's paid for, not what gets built.
