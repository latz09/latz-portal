02 · how it works — title/subtext LOCKED, steps below still DRAFT

How it works.

From the first conversation to a site that's live and yours — here's the path most projects follow.

1
Discovery

We start with a conversation — no pressure, just clarity. We talk through your business, your goals, and what's not working with your current site.

It's usually one conversation — sometimes that's all it takes to get moving, sometimes we dig in more. Either way, you walk away with a clear sense of what working together looks like.

2
Proposal & Blueprint

You get a clear, detailed quote — what's included, what it costs, how long it'll take. No jargon, no hidden fees, no surprises later.

The proposal comes with a blueprint — it shows what content lives on each page, not the final design. Nothing's locked. If we missed something, you flag it and we adjust before we start designing around it.

blueprint example
(rough structure/layout — sent with your proposal, the starting point for design)
If your project includes branding, that work happens here — before design starts — so everything after is built on a real brand, not guesses.
3
Design Directions

Starting from your blueprint, we create visual directions for your site. You react to each one — pick a favorite outright, or mix and match what you like across them.

That feedback becomes the foundation of your design system — colors, typography, buttons, headings — and we keep refining it together as the build comes together.

Direction A
Direction B
Direction C
4
Build & Preview

We build your Home page first with the design system in place, then bring you onto a live preview link — something like yourbusiness.latzwebdesign.com — a real website at a real URL, not a mockup. You react to it for real: colors, typography, buttons, all of it. When we're ready to launch, that same site just gets pointed to your domain.

From there, I keep building the rest of the site while our designer keeps designing the remaining pages — you watch it come together page by page, not all at once at the end.

first page (Home) — live preview
client gets link access here, keeps watching as remaining pages get built
SEO groundwork happens as part of the build, based on what we already know about your business from our conversations — titles, descriptions, structure, all of it. I'll send over a simple worksheet showing exactly what I went with for each page, so you can see it before launch and change anything you'd like — then, or anytime after, right in your CMS.
5
One Last Look

We do a final pass together before it goes live — copy, performance, every page, every device. Big layout changes get harder the closer we are to launch, but if something's bugging you, flag it and I'll see what's realistic.

6
Launch

Once you sign off, hosting, your CMS, and your domain all get moved into accounts in your name — real logins, nothing borrowed. You get a simple guide with screenshots showing exactly how to update the site yourself — most people pick it up in about five minutes.

Then I flip your domain over to the new site, and you're live. I personally register it with Google Search Console after launch and confirm it's actually getting indexed — not just assumed.
