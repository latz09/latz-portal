§01

Vision & Mission

Vision

To empower growing businesses with brands and websites they truly own — custom-built, conversion-focused, and designed to grow their business without hidden costs or unnecessary complexity. A website isn't just a brochure. It's the first handshake with a customer and should work 24/7 to earn trust and generate leads.

Mission

At Latz Web Design, we build custom brands and websites that are fast, stable, and simple to manage. Every project is designed to look great, work flawlessly on any device, rank well in search engines, and remain reliable without monthly maintenance fees or plugin headaches.

But we don't stop at design. We offer brand strategy that ensures every visual and messaging decision is intentional, and content management systems clients can actually use.

More than just delivering a website, our goal is to give each business a powerful growth engine they fully own.

§02

Core Values

Transparency

Clear communication and upfront pricing. No hidden fees, no surprise retainers, no fine print.

Ownership

Clients own their site, hosting, brand assets, and content outright. We hand everything over — nothing is held hostage.

Simplicity

A clean process, straightforward tools, and an editing experience that isn't bloated with clutter. Websites should be easy to manage, not a technical burden.

Performance

Every site is built fast, mobile-first, and SEO-ready — because speed and usability directly affect customer trust and conversions.

Integrity

No shortcuts, no templates, no reselling the same design. Each project reflects the unique identity of the client's business.

Direct Access

You talk to me directly — not a ticket system, not a chatbot, not whoever picked up today. One point of contact, from the first conversation to years down the road.

Target Audiences

Who You Serve Best

01Established Small Businesses
  • Already have a site (often WordPress or Wix) but it's outdated, slow, or hard to manage
  • They want credibility online and are ready to invest in something stable and modern
  • Pain point: Site doesn't reflect their brand, no way to track what's working, missing leads
  • Example: Local law firms, construction companies, boutique shops, family-owned businesses
02Professional Service Providers
  • Contractors, CPAs, healthcare providers, coaches, and consultants
  • They depend on inbound leads and need a site that builds trust and ranks well on Google
  • Pain point: Inconsistent branding, no visibility into which marketing actually works
  • They care less about "fancy templates" and more about performance, clarity, and ROI
03Growing Businesses Looking to Level Up
  • DIY owners currently on Wix, GoDaddy, or Squarespace
  • They've outgrown their drag-and-drop site and need something custom that reflects their brand
  • Pain point: Generic look, slow performance, inconsistent brand across touchpoints
  • They often come frustrated with "easy" platforms that ended up being limiting

  • Launching a business and need both brand identity and a website
  • Don't have a logo, colors, messaging, or web presence yet

  • Companies that want bundled marketing retainers (your model is project-first)

§08

Headless Commerce (Shopify)

For clients who need to sell products online without settling for a themed Shopify store. Same custom-built, client-owns-it model as every other Latz Web Design project — Shopify just handles the commerce layer.

How It Works

  • Custom Next.js frontend, built the same way as every other Latz Web Design site — no theme, no template
  • Shopify runs as the backend, handling what it's actually built for and battle-tested at: product catalog, inventory, secure checkout, and payment processing. A trusted, widely-used commerce engine — not a design system
  • The client gets a fully custom storefront with proven commerce infrastructure underneath — not a themed Shopify store customized on top

What This Solves

Off-the-shelf Shopify themes look like everyone else's and are clunky to customize. A themed store is still Shopify's design system wearing the client's logo. This flips that — the frontend is built custom, to the same speed, ownership, and design standards as every other build, and Shopify is doing what it's good at in the background instead of dictating what the site looks like.

Who It's For

Retailers and product-based businesses ready to move off a templated storefront — see Target Audience 05 (Businesses Selling Online) in Part I.

§09

SEO Foundations

The Core Idea

SEO isn't a checklist added at launch — it's wired into the CMS itself. Every project starts from the same template stack, and the SEO fields live in the CMS as content the client (or Jordan) can edit — title, description, business info, service areas — which automatically propagates to metadata, structured data, and social sharing across the entire site. Nothing is hardcoded per-page, and nothing goes stale because a plugin update broke it.

What's Built Into Every Project

  1. 01
    CMS-Driven SEO Settings

    One set of site-wide SEO data drives everything downstream: site identity and URL, fallback title and description, default social sharing image, and business/schema data. Edit it once, and it's reflected everywhere the site pulls from it.

  2. 02
    Metadata That Follows the Content

    Every page inherits consistent titles, descriptions, and branding automatically. Full Open Graph and Twitter Card data, plus the Google Search Console verification tag, are built into every project by default.

  3. 03
    Structured Data (Schema Markup)

    Every site ships with structured data (schema.org markup) telling Google exactly what kind of business it is, along with address, service areas, contact info, and price range where applicable. Google reads it — so do AI tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity.

  4. 04
    Sitemap & Robots.txt

    Automatically generated for every project — not hand-maintained, not forgotten.

  5. 05
    Search Console — Confirmed, Not Assumed

    The one manual step in the system, done personally by Jordan after every launch: register the site in Google Search Console, request indexing, and confirm it's actually being crawled and indexed correctly.

How This Is Different

WordPress Agencies

SEO usually depends on a plugin (like Yoast) layered on top — another thing that can break, conflict with other plugins, or fall out of date.

Wix / GoDaddy / Squarespace

SEO controls are limited and templated — clients get a form field, not a system.

Latz Web Design

SEO is infrastructure, built into the same CMS the client already uses to edit their site. Update the content, the SEO updates with it.

Client-Facing Copy

"SEO isn't something we bolt on before launch and hope holds up. It's built into the same system you use to edit your site — update your content, and your SEO updates with it. And when your site goes live, I personally register it with Google and confirm it's actually being found — nothing launches and gets forgotten."

Internal Note

This is bundled into every website build, not billed separately — it's infrastructure in the template stack itself, not incremental per-client labor. Clients don't need to "pay extra" for visibility (see §05). Content and keyword strategy — deciding what pages should exist, what keywords to target — is a separate discipline; it only shows up as a deliverable inside the Brand Strategy tier (§07). Ongoing SEO monitoring/reporting (rankings, traffic trends over time) is a real, distinct offering from this one-time foundation — a possible future add-on, not yet scoped. Revisit both alongside Pricing.

Internal Note — AEO / AI Search

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization — showing up in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews) is not a separate service line. The same technical SEO foundation above — structured data, sitemaps, clean metadata, Search Console indexing — is the same infrastructure AI tools read to identify and cite a business. Confirmed via Google Search Central's own AI-optimization guidance plus reporting on ChatGPT Search/Perplexity behavior. Folded in lightly: one mention on the structured-data item above, not a new section or claimed offering, and never a promise of citation or ranking outcomes. Service name stays "SEO Foundations" — not renamed — since nothing about the actual deliverable changed. Revisit if AEO ever becomes a real, separately scoped offering.

§10

Client Journey

Every project — website, branding, or both — moves through the same eight phases, from the first conversation to what happens after the site is live.

1
Discovery
  • We start with a conversation about your business, goals, and what's not working
  • This phase uncovers your audience, brand voice, and what success looks like
  • We determine if you need branding, a website, or both — and which tier fits
2
Proposal
  • You receive a clear, upfront project scope with pricing — no hidden fees
  • Timeline, deliverables, and expectations are fully outlined before we start
  • You choose your path: website only, branding only, or bundled together
3
Brand Foundation
if applicable
  • If doing branding work, this phase comes before design and development
  • We define your brand identity: logo, colors, fonts, voice, messaging, and any strategic deliverables
  • This ensures the website design is built on a real brand — not guesses
4
Design Directions
  • Our designer creates 1–3 visual directions (number depends on project scope)
  • You review each direction, share what you like and don't like, or pick a direction to move forward with
  • This ensures the final design matches your vision before we build
5
Build
with live link access
  • Your site is built from scratch — design, development, and copywriting
  • You're given a live staging link so you can watch the site being built in real time
  • This lets you give feedback throughout, ensuring alignment and avoiding big surprises
6
Review
  • We walk through the site together and refine until it's perfect
7
Handoff
  • Hosting and accounts are transferred into your name — you own everything
  • You get a full walkthrough on how to update your site (takes about 5 minutes to learn)
  • All brand assets are delivered in final file formats
  • We remain available for questions, but you're never locked into ongoing fees
8
Post-Launch
  • We personally confirm your site is registered with Google Search Console and actually getting indexed — not just assumed (full system in §09)
  • We ask for a review while the experience is still fresh
  • Analytics are already live — button-level tracking is set up in your own Vercel project from day one, so you can see what's actually getting used
Client-Facing Copy — Build Phase

"With a live link to your project, you can watch your site being built in real time. Instead of waiting weeks for a surprise 'reveal,' you'll see progress, give feedback, and stay involved at every step. It's transparent, collaborative, and ensures the final site matches your vision perfectly."

Internal Note

This stays narrative-level — what a client or new hire understands, not the operational tool. The real system underneath it is a Sanity-based client portal with 39 steps across 8 phases, tracking deposits, designer vs. internal assignment, and milestone due dates. Packaging the analytics data into a recurring client-facing report is a real idea Jordan's floated, not a committed deliverable — flagged for Pricing later.

§11

Sales Playbook

Discovery Questions

Use early in a call to uncover frustrations and goals you can anchor the rest of the conversation to.

  1. "What do you like and dislike about your current website?"
  2. "How do you update your site right now — and is that easy or frustrating?"
  3. "Have you had issues with your site breaking, being slow, or costing more than you expected?"
  4. "What role do you want your website to play — credibility, lead generation, sales tool?"
  5. "Are you paying ongoing fees now for maintenance, hosting, or updates?"
  6. "Do you have established branding — logo, colors, messaging — or is that something you need help with?"
  7. "If we could fix one thing immediately, what would it be?"

Objection Handling — WordPress

"Why not just use WordPress?"

"WordPress is powerful but depends on plugins that often clash or break with updates. Most of our clients came to us after their WP sites kept slowing down or costing them in retainers. We build fast, stable sites that don't rely on that bloat — and you own it outright."

"But plugins make WordPress flexible."

"Plugins can add features, but they also add risk. With our builds, you get the functionality you need custom-coded, so it's faster, safer, and longer-lasting."

Objection Handling — Wix / GoDaddy

"Wix is easier and cheaper — why not that?"

"Wix and GoDaddy are great for getting online fast, but most businesses outgrow them. They're template-based, limited in SEO, and you don't really own the platform. We build custom sites designed for growth, not just for being online."

"I already built on Wix — why should I change?"

"DIY builders work for a start, but if you want your site to bring in customers instead of just existing, you'll need something faster, more professional, and designed specifically around your business."

Objection Handling — Branding

"I already have a logo — do I really need branding?"

"A logo is one piece of the puzzle. Without a color palette, fonts, voice, and guidelines around it, your brand will look different everywhere it shows up. That inconsistency makes people trust you less, not more. We build the whole system so everything looks and sounds like the same business."

"Can't I just get a logo on Fiverr?"

"You can. And it'll look like it. We don't just design a logo — we build a brand system: colors, fonts, messaging, collateral, all designed to work together. Everything looks and sounds like the same business, everywhere it shows up."

"I don't think I need branding — just a website."

"Totally fine. Every website build includes brand-aligned copywriting and design. But if you ever want to take it further — full brand identity, business cards, messaging framework — we can add that anytime."

Budget Conversation Framework

Open the Door

"Budget's only awkward if we make it awkward. Do you have a ballpark in mind for what you'd like to invest in your website?"

If They Don't Know

"No problem — usually we work backwards. If you give us a range you're comfortable with, we'll work to maximize what we can do within that."

If They Give a Low Number

"You can absolutely find a website for a couple hundred dollars, but those are usually template-based sites that don't fix the problems you mentioned. If you want a site that actually works for your business, it makes sense to invest more."

Reframe the Value

"We build your site as a one-time project. Once it's done, it's 100% yours — hosting, code, and content. No monthly charges for the website itself. If you need branding too, we bundle it together for a better result and a better price."

Close the Loop

"Based on what you've told us, we can solve your problems and deliver a site that actually drives results — within that budget range."

§13

Brand Voice & Style

Core Voice Traits

  1. 01
    Direct & Confident

    Short, punchy lines. Say things plainly — "No fluff. Just results." Always sound like an expert who knows the value of their work.

  2. 02
    Client-Centered

    Focus on you (the client), not us (the team). Phrases like: "You deserve a site that works for your business — not the other way around."

  3. 03
    Anti-BS / No Gimmicks

    Call out the problems with WordPress/Wix directly. Lean into honesty: "No templates. No shortcuts. No ongoing fees."

  4. 04
    Results-Oriented

    Tie everything back to performance, leads, and ROI. Example: "Designed to convert. Built mobile-first, optimized for speed, and ready to rank."

  5. 05
    Trust & Ownership

    Emphasize that the client owns everything — the site, the hosting, the brand assets, and the tools. Example: "YOU OWN IT. FOREVER."

Voice Rules

  • Sentence length: keep it tight (5–12 words)
  • Style: use fragments for punch ("No fluff. Just results.")
  • Don't: use jargon ("bespoke," "synergy," "digital transformation")
  • Do: use everyday business language ("fast," "convert," "yours to own")
Internal Note

This voice governs every other client-facing section in this playbook — Sales Playbook scripts (§11), Messaging Framework lines (§12), proposals, and site copy. No jargon, no hedging, no corporate language creeping in anywhere client-facing.
