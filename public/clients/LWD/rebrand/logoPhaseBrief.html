Project Snapshot

The job

This one's for us, not a client. Latz Web Design has grown past the side project it started as — three real service lines now, a real voice, real positioning — but the logo is still the original Looka mark from day one. Before the new site and the full identity system come together, the mark needs to catch up to everything else that's already true about this business. Whatever direction lands here becomes the foundation the rest of the rebrand builds on.
