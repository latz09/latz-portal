Short Version

Custom-built websites and brands for small businesses — no templates, no monthly fees, clients own everything outright.

What We Do

Website builds (Next.js, Tailwind, Sanity, Vercel), branding & identity work — that's you — and headless commerce (Shopify as the backend, still fully custom-built). Three real service lines now, not just websites.

Who We Serve

Mostly Wisconsin/Midwest small businesses — established businesses stuck on an outdated WordPress or Wix site, service providers who live on inbound leads (contractors, CPAs, healthcare, coaches, consultants), businesses that outgrew DIY builders, and new businesses starting from scratch who need both a brand and a site.

What Makes Us Different

WordPress agencies leave clients stuck with plugins, updates, and retainers, with no real brand strategy behind any of it. Wix/GoDaddy sell "easy" sites that are generic and don't scale. We build custom, client-owned brands and websites — no lock-in, no monthly fees, built to convert.

Core Values

Transparency — upfront pricing, no hidden fees

Ownership — client owns everything outright

Simplicity — clean process, no bloat

Performance — fast, mobile-first, built to convert

Integrity — no templates, no shortcuts, no reselling designs

Direct Access — one point of contact, no ticket system, no chatbot

Voice

Direct, confident, no fluff, no jargon. Short and punchy over polished-corporate. Client-centered, not "we"-centered. Ownership is a big one — "YOU OWN IT. FOREVER." is a real line we use. A couple more in that spirit: "No fluff. Just results." "No templates. No shortcuts."

Quick Example of That Last Point

"We build custom websites for small businesses" (we-centered) vs. "You get a website you actually own" (client-centered) — same info, opposite focus. Matters most for taglines or any copy that ends up near the mark, not the mark itself.

The One-Liner

Custom brands and websites that are fast, stable, and fully owned by the client — built to convert visitors into customers.
