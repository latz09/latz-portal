What's Next for the Website

Six ideas to build on what's already live — for the club to talk through tonight, not decide tonight.

The site is live, membership payments work end to end, and Walter's already running the CMS. These six ideas are where it could go next. Nothing here is locked in, and there's no pricing in this version — tonight is about direction, not numbers.

A couple of these aren't separate projects on their own. #1 and #2 run off the same sponsor list behind the scenes, and #6 is what makes the automatic parts of #3 actually work. Worth keeping that in mind when talking about which pieces to move forward with.

What it is
A page listing every sponsor, with some getting their own dedicated page — their story, how they've supported the club, and a link back to their own site.
What it can do
Turns a sponsorship into something a sponsor wants to show off, not just a logo we were handed. We can share a sponsor's page on social media as a public thank-you.
What we could add
Make a dedicated page part of what a sponsor gets at a certain giving level — ties straight into the tiers in Become a Donor.
Questions for discussion
Does every sponsor get a page, or only certain levels? What goes on it — their story, photos, how much they've given?

What it is
The behind-the-scenes link between Square, where payments happen, and the website's own records.
What it can do
The moment someone pays — for membership or a donation — the website knows automatically. No more checking Square by hand to match up who's paid.
What we could add
The same connection can support features later on, like memberships that renew automatically each year.
Questions for discussion
Should the welcome or thank-you email go out automatically, or should someone review it first?

Where This Leaves Us

Nothing above has a price tag yet — that's on purpose. Once the club has a sense of which of these are worth pursuing, and the giving levels for Become a Donor, next comes real numbers and a clear plan for each one.
