Project Snapshot

The job

Redevelopment Resources has done real, named-client work for municipalities and developers across six states — and none of it shows on their current site. It's a 9.3-second-loading, plugin-heavy build from a vendor who's now upselling her weekly, the nav mislabels their actual services, and 44 project write-ups plus 14 named testimonials sit buried and unused. This rebuild fixes the structure, surfaces the real work, and gives a 165+-year firm a site that finally looks like one.

The 2 Reused Templates
Service Template — What All 5 Need

Case Study Template — What All 10 Need

  • — Related services — cross-links to the 1–2 most relevant service pages, plus a back-link to Our Projects

Let's Talk

☕
Bring your instincts — not just answers These are things worth talking through before we're deep in build mode. You know design better than I do — if you've got a direction you want to run with, let's hear it. These are conversation starters, not a checklist.
Open Design Questions
What's the color direction, given brand is unconfirmed?
Don't feel bound to the provisional navy/rust. I'd love to see 2–3 directions to kick off the conversation — including at least one that departs from what's there now.
Typography — credible without being stiff
Playfair Display / Source Sans Pro are placeholders, not a mandate. What pairing says "serious firm, real expertise" without reading like a law office?
How do we handle the gaps in photography?
The flagship story — arguably the best material on the whole site — doesn't have a hero photo yet. Texture, illustration, a strong color field, something else? Worth solving before we're stuck mocking up with a placeholder gray box.
How do 10 case studies avoid feeling like the same page 10 times?
One template, ten stories, real variation in what each one has to say. Where does the design earn variety — a stat layout, a quote treatment, an image ratio — without breaking the template itself?
How prominent should the flagship story be on Our Projects?
Confirmed it needs distinct treatment, separate from the grid. Full-width hero-style entry, or just a visibly larger grid card? Your call.
