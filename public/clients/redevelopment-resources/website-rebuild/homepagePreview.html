— What to look for

Layout first, everything else after.

This pass is about structure and flow, not final wording. Anything that looks unfinished is marking a spot on purpose.

01

Layout & Flow

How the sections connect, how it feels to scroll, and whether each page leads naturally to the next step, from your homepage all the way to getting in touch.

02

Words on the Page

Some copy is real and some is placeholder. The numbers on the homepage are working figures for now, so flag anything that should read differently and we'll lock in the real ones.

03

Images in Frame

Photos in place right now are holding space for size and mood. Now's a good time to think about which project photos and team headshots belong in each spot.

A short, planned hold so your feedback shapes the rest of the build instead of getting layered on top of it.

01

Look it over

02

Send your notes

A pause of up to three days

We hold new pages for up to three days to leave room for your feedback and keep launch on track. Meanwhile, the work keeps going behind the scenes:

  • Setting up your content manager, so you can update projects, testimonials, and team info yourself
  • Building the service page layout for Analysis, Strategy, Redevelopment, Financing, and Implementation
  • SEO groundwork, including redirects so links to your old project pages keep working
