Why this matters

You own this site, so the hosting should be in your name too, not billed through us. Moving it over doesn't touch the build or your launch timeline, it's simply a billing handoff. Once your card and billing address are confirmed, we'll connect redevelopment-resources.com and your site goes live.

Four Steps

1

Accept your invite

Check your email for an invite from Vercel to join the Redevelopment Resources team. Click through and create an account, or log into one you already have.

2

Add your card

In your new account, go to Settings → Billing and add a payment card.

Your first month of hosting is already covered, so nothing is charged right away.

That's everything on your end

Once your card, default payment method, and billing address are all set, we'll connect redevelopment-resources.com to the live site and you'll be ready to launch. From there, hosting is fully in your control, no monthly bill to us, no middleman.

One note: once the site is live, don't remove or let that card lapse, Vercel needs an active payment method on file to keep the site running.
