The Story
CMS managed
The building started as a hospital in Forest City, Iowa, then spent years as a law enforcement center before sitting vacant — owned by the county, with no clear future. When Redevelopment Resources took on the adaptive reuse, the real challenge wasn't construction, it was capital. We assembled a nine-source stack: the county contributed the building itself plus a grant, the city and state added further grants, and two competitive state tax credit awards closed the remaining gap.
Over two years, the building was reimagined as multifamily workforce housing. It's been fully occupied since November 2022 — proof that a building everyone else had written off can still work, with the right financing and the right patience. The next project already underway uses the same adaptive-reuse playbook: a former elementary school in Pleasantville, IA.
Full narrative — county-owned building history, funding stack, and the two-year timeline. This is the one canonical version of the Irish Hospital story — it lives here only, not duplicated anywhere else on the site.
