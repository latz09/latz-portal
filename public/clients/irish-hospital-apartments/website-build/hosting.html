Setting Up Your Own Hosting Account

Before your site goes live, we need to move hosting into your own account. Takes about 5 minutes.

Why this matters: You own this site, so you should own the hosting behind it too — no monthly fee routed through Jordan. This step doesn't touch the build or the timeline. It's strictly a billing handoff, and it takes effect once the site goes live on your domain.

That's it on your end. Once your card is added and set as default, hosting is set up in your account — your billing, your call, going forward. From here, we'll get the domain connected and the site live. One thing worth knowing ahead of time — removing this card or canceling the plan later would take the site offline, so if anything ever needs to change on the billing side, just reach out first and we'll sort out the right way to handle it.
