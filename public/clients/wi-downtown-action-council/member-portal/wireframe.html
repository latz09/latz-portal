Wisconsin Downtown Action Council — Member Portal

A new members-only space for your resource library — content & structure for board review, ahead of the Sept 17 meeting.

One login per member organization gives access to a searchable library of best practices, legislative updates, and other resources, with board members adding new content as it's ready. Linked right from your main site — your current pages stay exactly as they are.

✓Where Things Stand
🛠️ Working concept, not locked in. Everything below reflects where things stand today, based on our conversations so far — it's still a working concept, and anything can change as we get feedback from you and the board.
✓
A separate members' spaceThe portal is its own space, linked right from your main site. Your main site itself doesn't change — this isn't a rebuild of anything you already have.
✓
Org-level loginOne login per member organization, not per individual — built specifically around staff turnover at member orgs.
✓
Clean slate — nothing to migrateNo existing membership list. Membership used to turn over yearly with conference registration, so the portal launches fresh.
✓
PDFs plus optional linksDownloadable PDFs are the primary format we're planning around, with optional links out to other articles or videos. No video hosting in scope.
✓
Three fixed categoriesBest Practices, Legislative Updates, and Other Resources — every resource sorts into one of these three.
✓
Board members manage contentWDAC board members add resources, with possible part-time staff help later. Not Latz-managed, not Kristen personally.
