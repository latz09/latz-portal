Project Summary

Nothing is approved yet — the board decides Thursday, Sept 17 at 11am.

A wireframe and an informal proposal have been sent ahead of that meeting. The original $8,000–$9,000 low end feels too low once weighed against what the mockup actually shows: org-level accounts/login, a searchable and filterable resource library, and a self-serve "add a resource" form for board members. That's closer to a small custom app than a gated content page, and the price needs to move up accordingly — worth surfacing directly with Kristen rather than letting it come as a surprise after the board meeting.

Why They're Building This

  • 🔴 Received a "ridiculously high bid" from a previous vendor for this exact concept — soured on it, then came back wanting a real number
  • 🔴 No members-only content system exists today — best practices, legislative updates, and resources have no home
  • 🔴 Past "membership" was just conference registration, turning over every year — no durable member list or access system
  • 🔴 No way for board members to publish new resources themselves without going through a developer each time

What Success Looks Like

  • ✅ Each member org logs in once (shared org credential) and gets straight to a categorized, searchable library
  • ✅ WDAC board members add and publish new resources themselves — no developer involvement for routine content
  • ✅ The portal lives cleanly alongside the existing WordPress site with one outbound link — no disruption to what already works
  • ✅ Kristen walks into the board meeting with a real, defensible number instead of a guess
🚫
Explicitly not in scope No rebuild of WDAC's existing WordPress site — the portal is a separate subdomain app with one link out from the main site, which stays exactly as it is. No individual/per-person logins (org-level only, by design, for staff-turnover reasons). No native video hosting — only optional external links to articles or videos. No content or membership-list migration — WDAC has no existing roster to import, so this launches on a clean slate.

Scope & Pages

🗺️
Six pages, all new — the existing WDAC site is untouched The portal is its own space at a subdomain, linked from one new nav item on the current site. Nothing on wdac.org itself changes.
Public

Portal Landing — /member-portal

  • Public teaser: three resource-category cards (Best Practices, Legislative Updates, Other Resources) shown as a preview, not the real library
  • Login CTA — the actual content lives behind the login on Dashboard and Resource Library
Auth

Login — /member-portal/login

  • Org-level credential — one login per member organization, not per person
  • Provisioning method still open (see Needed from Client)
CMS

Dashboard — /member-portal/dashboard

  • Post-login home — category cards link into the pre-filtered library, counts update automatically as resources are added
  • "Recently Added" surfaces new resources as board members publish them
Shape Not Locked

Resource Library — /member-portal/resources

  • Search + category filter (Best Practices / Legislative Updates / Other Resources), CMS-editable
  • Option A — flat inline rows: title, category, direct download/visit action. Current lean — simpler, faster to build.
  • Option B — dedicated detail page per resource with its own URL: headline, body, optional embedded video, downloadable PDF. Stronger long-term SEO/search play.
Self-Serve

Org Account — /member-portal/account

  • Org name, contact email, and password are self-editable by the member org
  • Membership status is set on WDAC's side only — not self-editable, reflects whatever renewal process WDAC lands on
WDAC Admin Only

Add a Resource — /member-portal/resources/add

  • Where WDAC board members (and possibly part-time staff later) publish content directly — not Latz-managed, not Kristen personally
  • Form shape mirrors whichever Resource Library option is chosen: Option A is a quick PDF upload + short description; Option B is a full write-up with subheading, body, optional video link, and optional supplementary PDF
  • Published resources appear immediately in the library and on the Dashboard's "Recently Added" — no separate publish step

Not In Scope

Main Site Rebuild WDAC's existing WordPress site is untouched — gets one outbound nav link, nothing else changes
Individual Logins Org-level only, deliberately, due to staff turnover at member orgs
Video Hosting Optional external links only (e.g. YouTube/Vimeo) — no native video upload/hosting
Content Migration No existing member list or resource library to import — WDAC starts from a clean slate

Notes & Observations

Repricing — Say It Out Loud, Don't Let It Land as a Surprise

The original $8,000–$9,000 low end was set before the actual shape of the build was clear. The wireframe shows org-level auth, a searchable/filterable library, and self-serve publishing — that's a small custom app, not a gated content page. Kristen should hear the "why" behind the higher number directly, ideally before or during the board conversation, not as a line-item surprise in a follow-up invoice.

The "Ridiculously High Bid" Comment

Kristen led with this in her very first email. She's not price-shopping casually — she's been burned once already and is watching for it again. Frame pricing around what drives the number (real scope) rather than either lowballing to win the deal or getting defensive about the increase.

Zero Launch Content Is a Real Risk

Even once budget is approved and the five open decisions are locked, WDAC has no resources ready and the PDF template itself is still conceptual. A hard launch date could stall on content alone — worth proposing a soft-launch-with-minimal-content path so the build doesn't sit finished and unused.

Response Pattern

Kristen goes quiet for well over a week when something urgent (tax credits, other client work) takes over, then replies fully and promptly once she resurfaces. Follow-ups work — her Sept 15 reply came the same day as the nudge. Don't assume silence means the deal is cooling.

Sept 17 Board Meeting Is the Hinge Point

Nothing past pricing conversations moves until the board decides. Everything in Scope & Pages, Needed from Client, and Project Timeline above is written as the current working plan, not a committed build.
